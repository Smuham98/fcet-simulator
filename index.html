How the savings are built. Balance of plant scales with degrees removed. Interconnect and seal savings switch on once the stack runs at or below the threshold, where low-cost ferritic steel and simpler seals become usable. The power-density effect compares cell voltage at the chosen current density: the incumbent is a YSZ tape-cast cell at its own temperature, and a cooler cell with more resistance makes less power, so each kW costs more. Electrode ASR grows as the stack cools (Arrhenius, anchored at 600 °C) and becomes the main loss below about 600 °C, so the cathode has to be chosen for low temperature too. Stack-life value counts stack replacements avoided over the system's life. Electrolyte conductivity uses typical literature values (8YSZ ≈ 0.04 S/cm at 800 °C, Ea ≈ 1.0 eV; GDC10 ≈ 0.025 S/cm at 600 °C, Ea ≈ 0.7 eV), with 1.0 V at zero current. Not modeled: GDC electronic leakage, pinholes, gas crossover, interface resistance. Replace every anchor with FCET, PNNL or customer data before quoting.
